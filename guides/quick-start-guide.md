## Register on Direktt 📝

### **1. Go to the Registration Page**

* Open your browser and navigate to the **[Direktt admin console](https://direktt.com/wp-content/direkttweb/)**.

### **2. Fill Out the Registration Form**

* Enter a valid email address and a strong password
* OR, click **Login With Your Google Account** for quick access.

### **3. Confirm Your Email**

* Check your email inbox for a confirmation message from Direktt.
* Open the email and click the **verification link**.

### **4. All Done!**

* Once your email is confirmed, **[log in](https://direktt.com/wp-content/direkttweb/)** to start using Direktt.

## Create Your First Channel 📡

### **1. Add a New Channel**

* Click the **blue “Add New Channel”** button at the top right of the Channels section.

### **2. Enter Channel Name**

* A pop-up titled **Add New Channel** will appear.
* Type your new channel’s name in the **Channel Name** field.
* Click **Submit**.

### **3. Channel created!**

* Your new channel appears in the channel list – you’re ready to go!
