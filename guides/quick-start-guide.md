# 1. Register on Direktt 📝

### **Go to the Registration Page**

* Open your browser and navigate to the **[Direktt admin console](https://direktt.com/wp-content/direkttweb/)**.

### **Fill Out the Registration Form**

* Enter a valid email address and a strong password
* OR, click **Login With Your Google Account** for quick access.

### **Confirm Your Email**

* Check your email inbox for a confirmation message from Direktt.
* Open the email and click the **verification link**.

### **All Done!**

* Once your email is confirmed, **[log in](https://direktt.com/wp-content/direkttweb/)** to start using Direktt.
